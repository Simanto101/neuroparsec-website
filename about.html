About NeuroParsec

HealthTech,
built practically

NeuroParsec is a HealthTech company based in Dhaka, working worldwide. We build AI systems for the everyday work of healthcare: clinical notes, paperwork, follow-ups and training.

✚

What we do

We design and build working AI systems for clinics, hospitals and health teams, and customise each build for the organisation that will use it. Our current work includes clinical documentation, workflow automation and trainer enablement.

The Lab

NeuroParsec Lab is our research arm. Its first asset is the Human Atlas, a live 3D anatomy experience. The Lab is where new ideas are prototyped and tested before they become products.

How we work

Honest by default. Demos run on synthetic data and are labelled as demos. Concepts are labelled as concepts. Every automated system keeps a human in charge of the final decision.
