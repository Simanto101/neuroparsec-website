Second Brain

A local-first memory module with a 3D brain interface. It captures, organises and retrieves knowledge on the user's own machine, with a filter pipeline that turns raw material into detailed notes, then summaries, then filed knowledge with provenance.

Status: Built and running locally. Local-first by design: data stays on the device, and every filed item keeps a record of where it came from.
