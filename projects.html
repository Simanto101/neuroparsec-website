Working systems,
and what is next

Seven products below are working demos, built and test-verified on synthetic data. Six further designs are labelled In exploration: they are concepts, not finished products, and we say so plainly.

Working demos

Built and test-verified. Demos currently use clearly labelled offline, rule-based processing steps; each has a documented swap point where a live AI model connects in production.

Working demo

Smart Enquiry Handler

An automation that reads each incoming enquiry, understands what the person is asking, drafts a personal reply for a human to approve, logs the enquiry neatly, and sets a follow-up task with a due date based on urgency.

Problem: Enquiries arrive all day and are answered late, answered twice, or forgotten. Follow-ups depend on memory.

n8nWorkflow AutomationHuman-in-the-LoopClassification
Working demo

Meeting to Action List

A workflow that takes a meeting recording or transcript, in Bangla or English, and returns a short summary, the decisions made, and an action list with owners and dates. Every item quotes the exact sentence it came from.

Problem: Meeting minutes, decisions and promises rarely get written down, so the same points are discussed again next week.

n8nSpeech-to-TextSummarisationBangla and EnglishWorkflow Automation
Working demo

Inbox Triage Assistant

A workflow that reads incoming email, sorts it by type and urgency, routes each message to the right department, and drafts an acknowledgement reply for human review. Drafts are never auto-sent.

Problem: A shared inbox is a pile: a complaint can hide under job applications while everything waits for one person to sort it.

n8nEmail AutomationClassificationRoutingHuman-in-the-Loop
Working demo

Fair CV Screening Assistant

A workflow that screens job applications against a written role rubric and returns structured scores, a shortlist with reasons, and clear flags for human review. Every score shows its full breakdown.

Problem: Screening CVs by hand is slow and inconsistent, and black-box AI scores cannot be explained.

n8nHR TechTransparent ScoringHuman-in-the-Loop
Working demo

Weekly Content Pipeline

A workflow that takes one topic and produces drafted, formatted content for several channels at once, staged for human approval before anything is published. No statistics are invented, ever.

Problem: Teams know they should publish useful content every week, but writing several good posts from a blank page takes a full afternoon.

n8nContent AutomationMulti-Channel DraftingHuman Approval
Working demo

Prompt Engineering Trainer Kit

A complete, ready-to-teach training module on prompt engineering: a full trainer guide, session plan, hands-on labs, an assessment with a marking guide, a student handout, and a section on common student mistakes.

Problem: Many people can use AI tools, but far fewer can teach others to use them well, so course quality changes from batch to batch.

Training DesignPrompt EngineeringCurriculumAssessment
Working demo

Bilingual Clinic Scribe

A working demo that turns a doctor-patient consultation transcript, in Bangla and English mixed the way people actually speak, into a clean structured clinical summary, plus an honest list of what was not mentioned.

Problem: Doctors spend a large part of their day writing notes after consultations; rushed notes are hard to read, easy to lose, and often miss details.
